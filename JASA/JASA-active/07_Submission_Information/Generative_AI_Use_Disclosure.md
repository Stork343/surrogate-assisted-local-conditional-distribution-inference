# Generative AI Use Disclosure

Taylor & Francis requires the article to name each generative-AI tool and version, explain how it was used, and state the reason for use. This file separates AI that is part of the research design from AI assistance used during manuscript and code preparation.

## A. AI as a documented research instrument

DeepSeek models `deepseek-v4-pro` and `deepseek-v4-flash` were used as automated surrogate evaluators in the controlled synthetic AI-assisted review benchmark. They scored the synthetic dossiers under a common rubric before the expert-review samples were drawn. Expert-panel outcomes, rather than automated scores, defined the reference estimand. Model specifications, prompts, repeatability experiments, failures, and downstream analyses are documented in the Supplement and accompanying computational materials.

This research-instrument use is documented in Section 5 and the Supplement
because it is part of the research design and the resulting data-generating
mechanism.

## B. AI assistance in authoring and computational development

OpenAI Codex (GPT-5) was used during manuscript preparation for code debugging,
selective editorial revision, language refinement, and checks of wording,
consistency, citations, and typography. The authors reviewed and verified all
code changes and all revised text.
