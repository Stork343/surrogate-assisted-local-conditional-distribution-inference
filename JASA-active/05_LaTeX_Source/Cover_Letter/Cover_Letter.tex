Editors

\emph{Journal of the American Statistical Association}

Dear Editors,

We submit \textbf{``Surrogate-Assisted Local Conditional Distribution
Inference with Scarce Reference Outcomes''} for consideration in the
\emph{Journal of the American Statistical Association}, Theory and
Methods.

The paper studies the local conditional distribution

\[
F_0(y\mid x_0)=\Pr(Y\le y\mid X=x_0)
\]

when a reference outcome is observed through selective two-phase
verification, a lower-cost surrogate is broadly observed, and the
verification fraction may vanish jointly with localization. This
experiment arises in validation studies, sensor calibration, laboratory
confirmation, expert review, and model-assisted evaluation.

The paper makes three connected contributions. First, it derives the
observed-data canonical gradient and semiparametric efficiency bound for
a kernel-regularized conditional-CDF process, together with an exact
positive-semidefinite covariance-excess identity for alternative
augmentations under a known verification design. Second, it introduces
one process-wide borrowing coefficient. The resulting quadratic variance
criterion yields the transfer diagnostic \(2G_h-H_h\), the path-oracle
coefficient \(\Pi_{[0,1]}(G_h/H_h)\), and a three-way estimator that is
first-order equivalent to its fitted path oracle. Third, it establishes
Gaussian-process, multiplier-bootstrap, and conditional-quantile
inference when the reference fraction is fixed or vanishes, with
effective local information \(Mp_Mh^d\). The scarce-reference covariance
also yields a target-specific oracle square-root allocation benchmark.

The simulation study contains 119 configurations, including 24 settings
for simultaneous bands. It covers transfer geometry, fitted-path
adaptation, selective verification, scarcity, local reversal, estimated
verification probabilities, non-Gaussian and discrete outcomes, and
multivariate localization. Two real-data applications analyze EPA
air-sensor and VitalDB pulse-oximetry data; a controlled synthetic
AI-assisted review benchmark isolates selective review and local
surrogate reversal. Together they span strongly aligned, weak, discrete,
and locally reversed surrogate regimes.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and no
manuscript or substantially overlapping version has previously been
published, posted, presented, included in a thesis or dissertation, or
deposited in a public manuscript repository. The Supplement contains
complete proofs and extensions. The completed JASA Author Contributions
Checklist, code, and derived numerical data sufficient to reproduce
every reported table and figure accompany the submission.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
